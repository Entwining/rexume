After `task release`, Rexume now closes an unused disposable worker's pane that is the only pane of its session shown in a tab, as when the orchestrator and the other workers run elsewhere or detached. Rex gives such a pane focus whether or not an app shows its session, and Rexume used to put the close off while the pane had focus, so the pane stayed open. Rexume still puts the close off while the pane has focus and another pane of its session is shown in a tab.
